Generated by `python docs/plot_machines_suites.py`; data in docs/machines_suites.csv.

| Suite | Figure | Machines | Geomean speed-up of 8-lane SIMTiX (16-lane SIMTiX) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Applications (12) | docs/figs/machines_apps.pdf | CVA6, Ara, Vortex | CVA6 21.2x (32.9x), Ara 6.0x (8.9x), Vortex 4.1x (6.4x) |
| Suite 2 (10) | docs/figs/machines_suite2.pdf | CVA6, Ara | CVA6 14.6x (17.0x), Ara 3.4x (4.0x) |
| Rodinia (8) | docs/figs/machines_rodinia.pdf | CVA6, Ara, Vortex | CVA6 14.6x (23.8x), Ara 2.6x (4.3x), Vortex 2.0x (3.2x) |
| RiVEC (7) | docs/figs/machines_rivec.pdf | CVA6, Ara, Vortex | CVA6 20.2x (31.7x), Ara 3.8x (5.9x), Vortex 3.3x (5.1x) |

Speed-up = machine time / SIMTiX time, all times measured on the ZCU102 (SIMTiX 8 lanes at
115.37 MHz, 16 lanes at 100 MHz; CVA6 and Ara at 78.95 MHz; Vortex at 125 MHz). Each benchmark at
its largest board input (applications and suite 2 ran two sizes; the geomeans here are over the
plotted inputs, so they differ slightly from the paper's two-size means). Applications: SIMTiX's
best kernel variant, as in the paper (GEMM-RB, SpMV-Z, stencil-RB, k-means-U, segmented scan).

Not plotted: Ara MLP and Smith-Waterman (wrong outputs on the board), Ara Mandelbrot (deadlocks
Ara); Vortex has no suite-2 ports; the synchronization kernels (shuffles, block-shared memory)
exist on SIMTiX only. Suite-2 Black-Scholes on CVA6 and Ara (dagger): their host compares bit for
bit with SIMTiX's results and both report the same mismatch count (34,542 of 65,536 at the
smaller input), the signature of their shared divider's rounding, analysed for RiVEC
Black-Scholes in docs/rivec_results.md section 5 (largest change 5.7e-4 of the price).
