# A53 on the ZCU102, and SIMTiX against it (2026-10-28)

The application suite's A53 references measured on a ZCU102 (XCZU9EG, board
revision 1.1), and SIMTiX's cycles for the same inputs from simulation. Procedure
and tools: `sw/a53_zcu102/README.md`. Raw data: `docs/zcu102_a53_run_O2.txt`,
`docs/zcu102_a53_run_O3.txt` (the first run, before the arena hash, is
`docs/zcu102_a53_run.txt`), and `docs/board_sim.csv`.

## Setup

- One Cortex-A53 core (A53 #0), bare metal, MMU and caches on (standalone BSP).
  APU clock 1200.0 MHz from the clock registers (APLL FBDIV 72, DIV2, ACPU
  divisor 1); 1199.6-1199.9 MHz measured with the PMU cycle counter against the
  system counter.
- System counter (IOU_SCNTRS): 94.76-94.79 MHz measured against the host clock
  over 10 s in every run (IOPLL 1516.7 MHz / 16), not the 93.84 MHz that
  psu_init writes into its base-frequency register. All A53 times use the
  measured value.
- PS initialised over JTAG from U-Boot's `zynqmp-zcu102-rev1.1/psu_init_gpl.c`
  (SHA-256 69dcb39b...); DDR4 tested over the debug port before every run.
- References: `sw/ps_host/apps.c` built `-DSIMTIX_A53_ONLY`: ordinary sequential
  algorithms (i-k-j GEMM, a queue for BFS, a running sum for the scan), the same
  FP operations in the same order as the kernels (`fp-contract=off`, explicit
  `fmaf`, compiled to `fmadd`). Two builds: `-O2` (the board host's own build)
  and `-O3 -mcpu=cortex-a53` (GCC vectorises with NEON; 108 vector instructions).
  Neither uses `-ffast-math`.
- The arena is cleared at start, and an FNV-1a hash of the arena (inputs and A53
  results) after each application is the same in both builds for all 14 cases:
  the NEON build computes the same results.
- The two A53 runs agree with the first `-O2` run within 0.5%.

the rails are sampled between passes).

## SIMTiX against the A53 (medium inputs)

SIMTiX: `sim/board_sim.py --size board --lat 24`, the board top in xsim with an
AXI4 DDR4 model of 24 cycles of latency, 8 lanes, 4 warps, all outputs checked
bit-exactly; time at the board design's 100 MHz. **SIMTiX is simulated, the A53
measured.**

| Case | SIMTiX cycles | Warp instr. | IPC | Lane util. | L1 hit | SIMTiX at 100 MHz (ms) | Best A53 (ms) | SIMTiX / A53 time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bfs_16384x8 | 1,771,198 | 729,511 | 0.41 | 43% | 57% | 17.71 | 4.342 | 4.08 |
| gemm_64x64x64 | 337,591 | 241,152 | 0.71 | 100% | 93% | 3.38 | 0.675 | 5.00 |
| gemmrb_64x64x64 | 282,884 | 65,632 | 0.23 | 100% | 81% | 2.83 | 0.675 | 4.19 |
| kmeans_16384x16 | 993,810 | 714,752 | 0.72 | 100% | 96% | 9.94 | 2.147 | 4.63 |
| mlp_784x256x128x10_b16 | 1,973,694 | 968,992 | 0.49 | 100% | 52% | 19.74 | 46.113 | 0.43 |
| scan_65536 | 1,085,404 | 549,736 | 0.51 | 95% | 74% | 10.85 | 0.229 | 47.46 |
| spmv_4096x4096 | 263,749 | 89,080 | 0.34 | 59% | 75% | 2.64 | 0.475 | 5.55 |
| stencil_128x128x4 | 498,582 | 270,336 | 0.54 | 100% | 91% | 4.99 | 0.495 | 10.08 |

The A53 is 4-10x faster on six applications and 47x on the scan; SIMTiX is 2.3x
faster only on the INT8 MLP (`pdot8`). Reasons: 12x the clock, 1 MB of L2
against a 16 KB L1, many instructions per useful operation in the kernels, and
a single-slot FP unit (see `docs/v3_plan.md`, Findings). `gemmrb` is the
register-blocked GEMM of v3 phase A.
