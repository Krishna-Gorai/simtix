One figure per suite, generated by `python docs/plot_lanes_suites.py` from the per-case logs:

| Suite | Figure | Benchmarks | Speed-up over 4 lanes, geomean: 8 lanes | 16 lanes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Synchronization kernels | docs/figs/lanes_sync.pdf | 4 kernels x 3 sizes | 1.76x | 2.79x |
| Applications | docs/figs/lanes_apps.pdf | 17 | 1.84x | 3.26x |
| Suite 2 | docs/figs/lanes_suite2.pdf | 10 | 1.55x | 2.38x |
| Rodinia | docs/figs/lanes_rodinia.pdf | 8 | 1.71x | 3.11x |
| RiVEC | docs/figs/lanes_rivec.pdf | 7 | 1.67x | 2.88x |

Data: docs/lanes_scaling.csv (cycles at 4/8/16 lanes per case and the speed-ups).

## Method

Board-size simulation (Verilator, tests/tb_apps.sv on the board top simt_ps_top) of the board
configuration at each lane count, with the board's inputs and every output checked (54 cases,
all pass):

| | 4 lanes | 8 lanes | 16 lanes |
|---|---|---|---|
| warps, MSHRs, tags | 8, 16, 16 | 8, 16, 16 | 8, 16, 16 |
| L1 (2,048 lines each) | 32 KB, 16-B lines | 64 KB, 32-B lines | 128 KB, 64-B lines |
| divide/sqrt units | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| memory | AXI4 model, 24-cycle latency, late write responses | same | same |

8 lanes = the v3 board design; 16 lanes = the 16-lane board design (docs/lanes16_board_results.md);
4 lanes: the same family. Speed-up = cycles at 4 lanes / cycles at L lanes (the sum over a case's
kernel launches). Cases: sim/gen_lanes_board.py L (applications, suite 2, Rodinia, RiVEC) and
sim/gen_sync_board.py L (the host's four synchronization kernels at 1K, 16K and 256K threads, with
the host's inputs and reference results); runs: sim/run_lanes_board.sh L [suite...] with L1KB/SFU.

Agreement with the board (8 and 16 lanes, where both exist): the 8-to-16-lane cycle ratio of the
simulation matches the board's with a median error of 1.0 % on the 42 application/suite-2/Rodinia/
RiVEC cases (docs/lanes16_board_results.md); for the synchronization kernels within 7 %, except the
global-AMO histogram, whose 16-lane gain the simulation overstates by up to 13 % (1.60-1.71 vs
1.49-1.51 on the board).

## Notes

- amo_contend at 4 lanes: its 8-thread groups span two 4-lane warps, whose order is not defined,
  so the returned values and the amoswap results are order-dependent; at 4 lanes the check is
  restricted to the order-independent words (every amoadd sum and amomax maximum, exact: a lost
  atomic update would change them). Same kernel and inputs at every lane count.
- reduce_shfl at 4 lanes needs two shuffle steps: kernels/sync/reduce_shfl.S guards the third
  (8-lane image unchanged).
- tests/tb_apps.sv: LAUNCH lines take optional B and C bases (sync kernels); the poll limit was
  raised from 20M to 80M polls (RiVEC matmul 512 at 4 lanes runs ~75M cycles).
- Black-Scholes and NN are flat from 4 to 8 lanes (one divide/sqrt unit in both) and 2x at 16 (two
  units); hist_shared gains 2.2x from 4 to 8 lanes (at 4 lanes a 32-thread block needs all eight
  warps, so every block barrier stalls the whole machine); radix sort slows with more lanes
  (scattered stores move lines of one word per lane).
